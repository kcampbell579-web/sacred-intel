The Resource Conservation and Recovery Act, the law that governs hazardous waste from the moment it’s generated to the moment it’s buried, burned, or treated, is fifty years old. EPA marked the anniversary on October 21 with what it’s calling “Common Sense RCRA Reform”: a package of changes to permitting, treatment technologies, and cleanup speed. Three named actions, and a promise of more than 25 to come.

Electronic manifests only. A March 5 proposal would eliminate paper hazardous waste reporting in favor of the fully electronic e-Manifest. The manifest is the chain-of-custody document that follows every drum from generator to disposal; it’s how you prove your waste went where you said. Most large generators are already electronic. The small ones, the remediation contractors and demolition outfits that generate a few drums a year, aren’t all there, and this is a documentation change they’ll need to absorb.

Scrap tires as fuel. A March 23 proposal would let abandoned scrap tires be burned as fuel. Tire piles are a real problem; they breed mosquitoes and burn for weeks when they catch. Tire-derived fuel in a cement kiln is an established practice. The question is which kilns, with what controls, and what the air permits say.

Mercury treatment variance. Finalized June 26: a variance allowing novel treatment of high-mercury elemental mercury wastes at a US Ecology facility in Nevada. Narrow, technical, and the kind of thing that only matters if you generate that waste.

Generator status, manifesting, and recordkeeping live inside nearly every remediation and demolition compliance program. “Faster cleanups” is a phrase that sounds good and can mean a lot of things, some of which change what a site assessment has to look at. Watch the 25.

## What we know

- EPA announced the “Common Sense RCRA Reform” initiative October 21, 2026. Named actions: proposed elimination of paper reporting in favor of e-Manifest (proposed March 5, 2026); scrap tires as fuel (proposed March 23, 2026); mercury treatment variance finalized June 26, 2026. 25+ further actions in progress.

## What we don’t know yet

- The content and timing of the remaining actions.
